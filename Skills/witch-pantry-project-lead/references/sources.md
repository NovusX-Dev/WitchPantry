## Distilled Takeaways

- Research and prototyping should fail fast and answer uncertainty, not impersonate production.
- Vertical slice exists to test the full production pipeline on a representative slice.
- Feature lock on core verbs reduces downstream churn for content, UX, and marketing.
- Review cycles must adapt the plan based on evidence, not just report activity.
