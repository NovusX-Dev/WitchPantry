# Desktop Idler Mode

## Goal

Support a compact side-screen play pattern without making Witch Pantry feel stripped down, unreadable, or disconnected from the main pantry.

The compact mode is not a separate mini-game. It is a compressed view of the same magical workshop.

## Window Behavior

- compact layout should fit a narrow side or bottom desktop window
- core metrics stay visible at all times
- alerts should be concise and non-spammy
- animation should sell a living pantry without stealing attention from work or other play

## Minimum HUD

- gold or main progression currency
- primary demand or featured contract
- potion throughput or production health
- worst bottleneck
- room needing attention
- one to three quick actions

## Always-Glanceable Rules

- player should understand current state in under 5 seconds
- text must remain readable at compact scale
- top alerts should summarize action needed in plain language
- the player should know whether the pantry is healthy, blocked, or ready to claim
- room identity should remain visible even if full layout detail is compressed

## Reduced Interaction Model

Good compact actions:

- claim rewards
- fix a simple soft-friction event
- accept or complete a clear contract
- buy a suggested upgrade
- switch back to full management mode

Bad compact actions:

- drag complex layouts
- inspect long tables
- compare many recipes
- micromanage multiple rooms

## Success Criteria

- useful on a second monitor
- useful on the side or bottom of a large primary monitor
- not dependent on opening deep panels
- still clearly communicates the fantasy of a busy magical pantry
- encourages check-ins without demanding constant attention
